Kampong Basket One neighbourhood, one basket — nobody goes without.

How it works

A shared basket for the neighbourhood

Households who need daily groceries can list what they need. Neighbours who bought in bulk, got a supermarket discount, or received freebies can offer the extra. Volunteers match the two sides and keep everyone updated.

01

List it

Requesters (or a volunteer helping them) list the items they need. Donors list what they can spare.

02

Match it

Volunteers match urgent requests with available donations and message both sides.

03

Collect it

Items are dropped at the collection point, then handed over. Full items turn from grey to colour.
